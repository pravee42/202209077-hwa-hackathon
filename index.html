The quiet house problem

High-drive dogs don't misbehave because they're bad. They're under-stimulated — and every toy on the market gives up in the first 10 minutes.

⏱️

The 5-minute flaw

Treat puzzles, snuffle mats, and Kong-style toys expose every reward at once. Intelligent dogs crack the pattern or brute-force them in under 5 minutes — then stare at a silent house for the next 3 to 5 hours.

🦴

Cheatable designs

Lightweight dispensers flip upside down, migrate to the couch, or get knocked under furniture. Engagement ends prematurely — or becomes destructive chewing on slippers, doorframes, and cushions.

😰

No calm off-ramp

Electronic launchers and motorized gadgets break when chewed, scare noise-sensitive dogs, or spike adrenaline with zero wind-down — leaving dogs panting and anxious instead of tired and settled.

The solution

One SKU. Three modules. 3+ hours covered.
